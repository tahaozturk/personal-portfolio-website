Two-seat sky racing

One flies. One fires.

Every racer has two seats. Take the pilot's stick, the gunner's guns, or both at once, and race low and fast through canyons, forests, islands and storm coasts. Solo against AI, or crewed up with a friend online.

Free. No ads, no pay-to-win: everything is earned by racing.

Pick your seat

Pilot, gunner, or both

Your AI crewmate takes whichever seat you leave. Human and AI play by the same numbers: same damage, same reload, same lock time.

The ship is heavy and slow to answer the stick. Steer into every bend, boost on the straights, and raise the shield when a missile closes in.

Race by your wits

Win by any means

Nobody gets hurt for good: ships at zero hull are knocked out for a few seconds, then recover and rejoin the race.

Co-op crews

Team up with a friend: one flies, one fights. Play over the internet with a crew code, or on the same Wi-Fi with no account needed.

A real career

25 levels across five leagues. Earn credits, unlock courses, and buy faster ships and heavier guns in the garage.

Slipstream and slingshot

Tail a rival to fill the slipstream bar, then pull out of the wake to slingshot past. Fast ships win the straights, nimble ones the bends.

The nuke

One shared nuke pod hides somewhere on every course. Grab it first and send it after the leader. The shield can't stop it; only gunfire can.

Leader draws the fire

Every rival gunner favours whoever leads, and sends long-range missiles after them. Taking first place means taking all the heat.

Speaks your language

Original music, every model built in code, and a race announcer who calls the action in all eight interface languages.
